The Engelsystem includes German (`de_DE`) and English (`en_US`) translations.

## Overriding translations

Override specific translations by creating a `config/lang/<locale>/custom.po` file
(e.g., `config/lang/de_DE/custom.po`).

Alternatively, use a custom
[Translation Mapper](https://github.com/MyIgel/engelsystem/commit/0a5c629deaacbb10d11673918c3579a8c867a695)
to replace text strings after translation.
